Food Delivery System (Swiggy/Zomato)

Requirements
Users can browse restaurants
Add items to cart and place orders
Restaurants accept/reject orders
Assign delivery partner
Track order status
Handle payments
Maintain order history

Entities - 

User, 
Partner,
Restaurant,
RestaurantManager,
Item, 
Cart, 
Order, 
OrderManager, 
Payment, 
AssignPartner, 
AssignPartnerStrategy (abstract class),
NearestPartnerStrategy,
HighestRatedPartnerStrategy

Relationships - 

User has order history, cart
Partner has order history
RestaurantManager has list of restaurants,
Restaurant has list of items,
Cart has list of items
Order has user, restaurant, cart, payment, ORDER_STATUS, startTime, duration
OrderManager has orders, AssignPartner
AssignPartner has AssignPartnerStrategy

Methods - 

User - browseRestaurant(drop location), placeOrder(cart), completePayment(Payment)
Partner - startDelivery(order), completeDelivery(order), goOffline()
RestaurantManager - findRestaurants(location)
Restaurant - takeOrderRequest(Order)
Cart - addItem(Item), removeItem(Item), calculateCost()
OrderManager - createOrder(cart), updateOrderStatus(order, status)
AssignPartner - assignPartner(order)

Attributes - 

User - id, Cart, vector<Order*>
Cart - vector<Items*>
Partner - id, PARTNER_STATUS, currLocation, vector<Order*>
RestaurantManager - vector<Restaurant*>
Restaurant - id, location, vector<Items*>
Item - id, name, price
Order - id, user, drop, restaurant, cart, startTime, duration, partner
OrderManager - vector<Order*>, AssignPartner
Payment - id, amount, PAYMENT_STATUS
AssignPartner - AssignPartnerStrategy
