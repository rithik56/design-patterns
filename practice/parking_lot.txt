Parking Lot System

Requirements
Multiple parking floors
Different vehicle types (Car, Bike, Truck)
Allocate nearest available slot
Track entry and exit time
Calculate parking fee based on duration
Support multiple payment methods
Show available slots per floor

Entities - 

ParkingFloor, 
ParkingManager, 
Vehicle, 
Slot,
TicketManager,
Ticket,
PaymentManager, 
Payment (abstract class), 
UPI (Payment), 
Card (Payment)

Relationships - 

ParkingFloor has list of Slots,
ParkingManager has list of ParkingFloors, 
TicketManager has list of tickets
Ticket has vehicle, slot
PaymentManager has list of payments

Methods - 

ParkingFloor - showAvailableSlots()
ParkingManager - allocateSlot(Vehicle)
TicketManager - createTicket(Vehicle, slot), calculateFees(ticket)
PaymentManager - createPayment(PAYMENT_METHOD, ticket)
Payment - processPayment()

Attributes - 

ParkingFloor - id, vector<Slots*>
ParkingManager - vector<ParkingFloor*>
Vehicle - vehicleNumber, vehicleType
Slot - id, SLOT_STATUS
TicketManager - vector<Ticket*>
Ticket - id, vehicle, slot, entryTime, exitTime, fees, PAYMENT_STATUS, paymentId
PaymentManager - vector<Payment*>
Payment - id, amount, PAYMENT_STATUS
